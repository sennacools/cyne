About us

Timeless spaces with a character of their own

What we do

At CYNÉ, we create timeless spaces with a character of their own. Our work spans interior architecture, spatial design and scenography.

Each project begins with the people who will experience it. A glimpse into a courtyard, the curve of a screen or the movement of a stage set can become the detail they remember.

Cygne de Lune
